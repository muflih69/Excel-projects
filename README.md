# **SUPERMARKET SALES ANALYSIS**

## **ABOUT THE PROJECT**

This project analyzes supermarket sales data using **Microsoft Excel** to understand sales performance, customer behavior, product performance, and payment preferences.

---

## **BUSINESS QUESTIONS**

1. Which branch generates the highest total sales?
2. Which product line generates the highest sales and sells the most quantity?
3. How do Member and Normal customers differ in their spending?
4. Which payment method is most commonly used and generates the highest sales?
5. Is customer rating related to sales performance?

## **KEY FINDINGS**

| Area | Finding |
|---|---|
| 🏢 Branch | Brooklyn generated the highest total sales — **319,705.48** |
| 🛍️ Product Line | Home & Lifestyle generated the highest sales — **197,260.56** |
| 📦 Quantity | Food & Beverages had the highest quantity sold — **7,309 units** |
| 👥 Customer Type | Members had a higher average sales value — **209.29** |
| 💳 Payment | Ewallet generated the highest sales — **326,691.31** |
| ⭐ Rating | Poor-rated transactions had the highest average sales — **219.60** |

---

## **BUSINESS RECOMMENDATIONS**

- Study successful sales practices in Brooklyn and consider applying them to other branches.
- Maintain sufficient Food & Beverages inventory and promote Home & Lifestyle products.
- Encourage Normal customers to join the membership program.
- Continue supporting Ewallet payments and reliable digital-payment services.
- Investigate the reasons behind poor customer ratings, especially for higher-value purchases.
